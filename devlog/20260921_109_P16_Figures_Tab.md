## What

A fifth tab, **Figures**, between Text and References: the paper read by
its figures. One block per figure or plate in page order —

1. the **whole figure**, up to 760 px wide, with a box on every sub-figure
   the split stage found, in the panel colours;
2. under it, the **caption entries** the linking stage cut, one line each,
   in their panel's colour; panels with no entry and entries with no panel
   after them, greyed; a figure without entries shows its caption, one
   without a caption says so.

The cursor ties the two: **over a box, its entry fills yellow** (and scrolls
into view); **over an entry, its box lights** — yellow, the rest faded. A
click keeps the pairing. The Text tab's figure list and the split view under
it (108) stay as they were.

## How

- `FigureBlock(row)`: `FigureCanvas` (from 108, now with `panel_hovered`,
  `hover()`, and per-instance size limits) plus `_EntryList`, a `QListWidget`
  with mouse tracking whose `entered` signal names the row; `mark_hover()`
  fills a row's background. The block maps entry row ↔ panel index.
- `FiguresTab(QScrollArea)`: builds every block at once (they are cheap:
  labels and an empty canvas sized from the figure's page box), then
  **renders lazily**. `_render_visible()` runs on scroll (120 ms debounce)
  and once after the build: blocks within 600 px of the viewport are
  requested from one `_CropWorker` (page render at a dpi that makes the crop
  ~900 px wide, then the crop); blocks farther than 2 400 px let their crop
  go; at most 10 crops are held, the oldest off-screen one released first.
  So a 121-plate paper costs the pages you look at, not the volume.
- One thing the scroll area does a turn late: sizing the host to its
  content. The first `_render_visible` after a build would see every block
  at y = 0 and request them all — so it activates the layout and resizes
  the host to its minimum size hint before judging positions.
- `DetailPanel`: `_figures_wrapper` built on first activation like the other
  lazy tabs; tab indices in `_on_tab_changed` shifted (Figures 3,
  References 4).

## A test-isolation bug found on the way

`test_config_location` and `test_paths` re-import `papermeister.*` to
re-resolve paths, by deleting the modules from `sys.modules`. Anything
imported earlier — the desktop services — keeps the old model classes, bound
to the old peewee proxy, which the next `init_db` does not initialize; the
first DB-touching UI test after them failed with "Cannot use uninitialized
Proxy" (only in the full run, never alone). `conftest.py` now has an autouse
fixture that puts the original modules back after each test and re-points
every package attribute, so a later `from papermeister import x` sees the
same module the services hold.

## Addendum (same day): tooltips, caption tabs, width-fit, proportional panels

- **Hovering a box** shows its entry as a tooltip (`FigureCanvas` tips,
  `entry_text(panel)`), in the Figures tab and the Text tab's split view.
- Under each figure the caption is on two small tabs — **Entries (N)** (cut)
  and **Caption** (as printed) — when both exist.
- **Width-fit**: the Figures tab's canvases follow the viewport width
  (`figure_width()`, re-fitted on resize with a 200 ms debounce; crops are
  requested at 1.5× that width and rescaled at paint). The PDF tab's
  `_LazyPdfView` now fits the widest page to the viewport instead of a fixed
  1.5× zoom, re-fits on resize and decodes at the device pixel ratio. Both
  keep the vertical scrollbar always on: a scrollbar appearing after the
  first fit narrowed the viewport and decoded every page twice.
- **Main window splitter**: stretch factors 1 / 2 / 2 (were 0 / 1 / 0), so a
  wider window widens the detail panel too, not only the paper list.
- Bruton 2004 (1737) and Naimark 2006 (1541) had their panels split (13 of
  14 plates; one `duplicate_caption_index`); Bruton Plate 1 renders with a
  box on each of its 16 specimens.

## Addendum 2 (same day): the Text tab is the text again

Reviewing the Figures tab, the user asked for the Text tab to show figures
only inline, where they fall in the text, and for every detail — list,
boxes, entries — to live in the Figures tab. So the figure list (107), the
split view under it (108) and the boxes drawn over the reader's figures
(108, step 3) are gone; `panel_tiles.py` and `figure_list.py` with them.
`FigureCanvas`, `CropWorker` and `entry_text` moved to
`desktop/components/figure_canvas.py`. `load_figures`' counts and
`load_panels` / `load_entries` stay as the Figures tab's data.

Also from the same review: **one quiet colour** for all panel boxes
(`#7b93ad`, 1 px, label tag translucent) — the plate is what the eye should
be on; only a hovered or chosen panel is yellow. Entries are no longer
coloured per panel (greyed only when no panel claims them).

And a validator fix: Naimark 2006 Fig. 2 pairs each specimen's photograph
with its outline drawing, so entry (a) rightly sits on two panels; the
check rejected that as `duplicate_caption_index`. It is now a review reason,
`entry_on_several_panels` (the same index twice on *one* panel is still a
fault). Re-collected: 1541 Fig. 2 (6 panels) and 664 Fig. 11 (9 panels)
landed. `split_panels.py --figure-ids N --force` re-asks the server past its
reply cache when a rejected item needs a fresh answer.
